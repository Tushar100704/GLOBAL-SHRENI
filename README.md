## 🌍 The Idea

> **Talent exists everywhere. Opportunity doesn't.**

Global Shreni is being built to bridge that gap.

It is a unified marketplace designed to connect:

**People · Skills · Services · Businesses · Opportunity**

across both the **digital and physical economy**.

Unlike marketplaces focused primarily on remote digital work, Global Shreni aims to bring structured discovery and collaboration to the **offline economy as well** — connecting local artisans, skilled workers, service providers, freelancers, professionals, and businesses within one ecosystem.

---

## ✦ What is Global Shreni?

Global Shreni is designed as a marketplace where people can:

- Discover relevant talent
- Find professional services
- Find local skilled workers
- Publish requirements
- Connect with businesses
- Discover opportunities
- Collaborate on engagements
- Build professional reputation

The long-term goal is simple:

> **Make every skill easier to discover and every opportunity easier to access.**
