What is Global Shreni?+

A unified marketplace concept connecting skilled people, services and businesses across online and offline work.

Who can join?+

Freelancers, professionals, artisans, skilled workers, service providers, customers and businesses can participate as the marketplace expands.

Can work happen offline?+

Yes. Offline and hybrid engagements are central to the Global Shreni concept alongside remote digital work.

How does reputation work?+

The platform is designed to combine meaningful signals such as verification, reviews, completed work and professional history.
