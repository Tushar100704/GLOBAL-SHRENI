A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Questions, answered.

Who can join Global Shreni?+

Freelancers, local professionals, artisans, skilled workers, businesses, and people offering flexible help can create a profile.

How are people matched?+

Profiles are ranked by category, skills, location, availability, and reviews from completed work. Employers make the final selection.

Can I post work that needs no formal qualification?+

Yes. The guild supports short day contracts for general tasks. Employers describe the work, hours, and pay before workers apply.

How do payment and verification work?+

Agree on payment directly with the other person. In this MVP, Global Shreni does not hold payments or perform identity checks.
